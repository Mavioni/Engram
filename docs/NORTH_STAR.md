# Engram — North Star

## The irreducible why

Engram exists so a person can notice themselves clearly,
in private, without being interpreted by anyone else —
including the software.

## First-principles constraints (binding, non-negotiable)

1. **ISO doctrine.** Engram never speaks as the user, never
   completes their sentences, never answers on their behalf.
   IRIS reflects, asks, and witnesses. The user remains the
   sole author of self. Violating this collapses the product.

2. **Privacy is the substrate, not a feature.** Journal
   entries, check-ins, and chat content are the user's. They
   are encrypted at rest, scoped to the user's account, and
   never trained on without explicit, revocable consent.

3. **The Engram card is a mirror, not a verdict.** It is
   IRIS-derived reflection of what the user expressed —
   re-takeable, owned, never sold as a "type."

4. **Embodiment-portable output.** Every IRIS turn, mood
   sample, journal entry, and insight is structured state,
   not chat-string ephemera, so the same signals can drive
   future native surfaces (voice, ambient, wearable, robot)
   without a rewrite.

5. **Critical path only.** A feature ships only if removing
   it breaks the loop: check-in → reflection → insight →
   card evolution. Streaks-as-pressure, social leaderboards,
   and ranking are out of scope by construction.

## The commercial path, reduced

A subscription on App Store + Play Store is viable iff a
paying user, on day 30, can truthfully say: "this changed
how I notice myself." Retention is the only honest metric;
engagement-bait is forbidden.

## What Engram is not

Not a therapist. Not an oracle. Not a personality test.
Not a coach that knows better. Not a feed.

It is a quiet instrument the user holds.
